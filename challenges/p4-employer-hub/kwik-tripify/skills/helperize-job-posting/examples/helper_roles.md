## Role 1: Store Care Helper (weekday mornings)

Keeps the sales floor clean and clear: sweeping and mopping, emptying trash, and breaking down boxes after stocking. Associates can stay on the register and with customers during the morning rush.

- **Hours:** 10-15 per week; 3 weekday mornings, about 9:00-12:00, set around Madison Metro or paratransit times
- **Pay:** same $16-18 starting range as other associates
- **Support intensity (of the role):** light
- **Frees up:** associates who leave the register to sweep, empty trash, or clear boxes
- **Growth path:** add facing shelves (t5), then restroom cleaning with a cleaning log (t7), then stocking (t4)

| Task | Standard (what done looks like) | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sweep and mop sales floor | no debris; wet-floor sign out until dry | twice per shift |
| Empty trash cans | cans under half full; new liner in | every 2 hours |
| Break down cardboard boxes | boxes flat and stacked by the baler | after stocking |

**Executive-function demands (role level)**

| Dimension | Demand |
| --- | --- |
| working memory | low |
| sequencing | low |
| time management | low |
| attention | low |
| organization | low |
| flexibility | low |
| social communication | low |
| sensory load | low |
| paperwork | none |

**Supports the employer agrees to provide**

- picture checklist of the cleaning route at the supply closet
- phone timer for the every-2-hours trash round
- one named coworker to ask when supplies run out
- job coach for the first week, then fading

_Why these tasks belong together:_ all floor-care tasks on one route, one supply closet, done in the quieter morning

**Variations of this role (tasks and schedules can be mixed)**

- **Core:** Empty trash cans — 2 h/day x 2 days = 4 h/week total (two weekday mornings) or 2 h/day x 3 days = 6 h/week total. Support intensity: light. Good fit: prefers one task at a time on a fixed route and a short shift.
- **Standard** = Core + Sweep and mop sales floor; Break down cardboard boxes — 3 h/day x 3 days = 9 h/week total or 5 h/day x 3 days = 15 h/week total. Support intensity: light. Good fit: ready for three floor-care tasks on one route.
- **Plus** = Standard + Face shelves — 5 h/day x 3 days = 15 h/week total or 5 h/day x 4 days = 20 h/week total. Support intensity: light. Good fit: has mastered floor care and wants to add facing shelves.

_Schedules above 15 hours a week, and any schedule a specific client takes, go to an ERI benefits counselor first._

## Role 2: Front Lot and Welcome Helper

Keeps carts flowing from the lot to the entrance and greets customers at the door, pointing them to aisles or handing questions to a coworker.

- **Hours:** 10-15 per week; weekday late mornings into lunch, when cart traffic is highest; daylight only
- **Pay:** same $16-18 starting range as other associates
- **Support intensity (of the role):** moderate
- **Frees up:** cashiers called out to the lot when carts run low
- **Growth path:** add facing shelves (t5), then expiration checks (t12), then register backup only if the person wants it

| Task | Standard (what done looks like) | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bring carts in from the parking lot and organize the corral | no more than 5 carts in the lot; corral rows straight | every 30 minutes |
| Greet customers and point them to products | customer greeted within a few seconds; directed to the right aisle or handed to a coworker | throughout shift |

**Executive-function demands (role level)**

| Dimension | Demand |
| --- | --- |
| working memory | low |
| sequencing | low |
| time management | low |
| attention | medium |
| organization | low |
| flexibility | low |
| social communication | medium |
| sensory load | medium |
| paperwork | none |

**Supports the employer agrees to provide**

- parking-lot safety training and a high-visibility vest
- short greeting script and a store map card for product questions
- a buddy at customer service to hand harder questions to
- job coach on site for the first weeks; check-ins every 2 weeks
- breaks in a quiet area; weather gear provided

_Why these tasks belong together:_ both tasks sit at the front entrance and share the same busy window

**Variations of this role (tasks and schedules can be mixed)**

- **Core:** Bring carts in from the parking lot and organize the corral — 2 h/day x 2 days = 4 h/week total (two weekday late mornings) or 2 h/day x 3 days = 6 h/week total. Support intensity: moderate. Good fit: likes moving around outdoors and prefers little customer conversation.
- **Standard** = Core + Greet customers and point them to products — 3 h/day x 3 days = 9 h/week total or 5 h/day x 3 days = 15 h/week total. Support intensity: moderate. Good fit: comfortable with short, scripted greetings between cart rounds.

_Schedules above 15 hours a week, and any schedule a specific client takes, go to an ERI benefits counselor first._

## Role 3: Shelf and Freshness Helper

Keeps shelves full-looking and safe: faces products forward and pulls expired items from a set list of aisles each day.

- **Hours:** 10-15 per week; weekday mornings after the delivery is stocked
- **Pay:** same $16-18 starting range as other associates
- **Support intensity (of the role):** moderate
- **Frees up:** stockers and managers who check dates at closing
- **Growth path:** add stocking with date rotation (t4), then price tags (t13)

| Task | Standard (what done looks like) | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Face shelves | front row full; every label faces out | each aisle twice per shift |
| Check expiration dates and pull expired items | no expired items on the shelf; pulled items logged | daily, set aisles |

**Executive-function demands (role level)**

| Dimension | Demand |
| --- | --- |
| working memory | low |
| sequencing | low |
| time management | low |
| attention | medium |
| organization | medium |
| flexibility | low |
| social communication | low |
| sensory load | low |
| paperwork | medium |

**Supports the employer agrees to provide**

- aisle-by-aisle route card for each day
- picture guide to reading date codes, with a magnifier if wanted
- picture log or a scan-only log for pulled items
- supervisor sets which aisles to check each day
- job coach on site for the first weeks; check-ins every 2 weeks

_Why these tasks belong together:_ both tasks walk the same aisles and use the same 'shelf looks right' standard

**Variations of this role (tasks and schedules can be mixed)**

- **Core:** Face shelves — 2 h/day x 2 days = 4 h/week total (two weekday mornings) or 3 h/day x 3 days = 9 h/week total. Support intensity: light. Good fit: wants to learn the aisles by facing before checking dates.
- **Standard** = Core + Check expiration dates and pull expired items — 3 h/day x 3 days = 9 h/week total or 5 h/day x 3 days = 15 h/week total. Support intensity: moderate. Good fit: comfortable reading date codes with a picture guide.
- **Plus** = Standard + Stock shelves, coolers, and freezers, rotating by date (FIFO) — 5 h/day x 3 days = 15 h/week total. Support intensity: intensive. Good fit: ready to stock with date rotation.

_Schedules above 15 hours a week, and any schedule a specific client takes, go to an ERI benefits counselor first._

## Questions to confirm with the employer

- [ ] Is 'lift up to 50 lbs' essential for every associate, or only for unloading trucks? The helper roles carry lighter loads.
- [ ] Can these roles be weekday daytime only? The posting asks for evenings and weekends.
- [ ] Confirm the starting wage for helpers is the same $16-18 range as associates.
- [ ] Is 'strong multitasking in a fast-paced environment' needed for these roles, or only for register work?
- [ ] Who supervises each role, and who is the backup?
- [ ] What cleaning chemicals are used, and what safety training do all staff get?
- [ ] Does the parking lot have a safe walking path for cart retrieval?
- [ ] How are pulled expired items logged today?
- [ ] Would you start with one person in one role, then decide on more?
