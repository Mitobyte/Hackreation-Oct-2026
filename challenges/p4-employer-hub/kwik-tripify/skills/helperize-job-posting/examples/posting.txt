Title: Grocery Store Associate
Employer: Lakeside Market (fictional example)
Location: Madison, WI
Employment type: PART_TIME
Pay: 16-18 HOUR

Lakeside Market is hiring friendly Grocery Store Associates to keep our store running smoothly.
What you will do
- Greet customers and answer questions about where products are located
- Operate the cash register, handle cash and card payments, and process returns
- Unload delivery trucks and stock shelves, coolers, and freezers, rotating product by date (FIFO)
- Face shelves so products are pulled forward and labels face out
- Retrieve shopping carts from the parking lot and keep the cart corral organized
- Clean restrooms, sweep and mop floors, and empty trash on a set schedule
- Break down cardboard boxes and run the baler
- Check expiration dates and pull expired items
- Help with price changes and put up shelf tags
- Resolve customer complaints and call a manager when needed
- Open or close the store, including counting the cash drawer
Requirements
- Able to lift up to 50 lbs and stand for a full shift
- Flexible availability including evenings and weekends
- Strong communication and multitasking skills in a fast-paced environment
