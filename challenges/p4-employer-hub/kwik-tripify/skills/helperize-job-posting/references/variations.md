# Variations: versions of a role for different people and schedules

Every role gets at least two variations. A variation changes **which tasks** and **how many days and hours**, so a job developer can match the role to a person without rewriting it. Describe each variation by its tasks, demands, and supports; the "best when" line names working preferences ("prefers one task at a time"), never a diagnosis or label.

## Task versions

| Version | Tasks | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Core** | The one or two lowest-demand tasks of the role (lowest support intensity) | Someone wants a short, single-routine job, or is new to work |
| **Standard** | All tasks of the role | The role as carved |
| **Plus** | The role's tasks plus the next growth-path task, if it is not keep-with-staff and not rated 3 | Someone has mastered the standard version or wants more variety |

Skip Plus when no eligible growth task exists. Add a Swap version when two tasks of similar demand can trade places (for example, indoor cleaning instead of cart retrieval for someone who prefers indoor work).

## Schedule ladder

Offer schedules from this ladder that fit the employer's busy times. Kwik Trip started helpers at up to 15 hours a week ([RespectAbility](https://www.respectability.org/respecttheability-campaign-spotlight-on-kwik-trip)).

| Step | Pattern | Hours a week | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | 2 days x 2 hours | 4 | First job, short stamina, or building routine |
| Short | 2 days x 3 hours, or 3 days x 2 hours | 6 | Building up |
| Part | 3 days x 3 hours | 9 | Steady routine |
| Standard | 3 days x 5 hours, or 5 days x 3 hours | 15 | The Kwik Trip start |
| Growth | 4 days x 5 hours | 20 | After the person and employer agree, and after an ERI benefits counselor reviews it |

Every role's variations together must include at least one schedule of 6 hours a week or less.

Hours and pay can change a client's SSI, SSDI, or Medicaid. Every schedule here is an option; an ERI benefits counselor confirms the one a client takes.

## Writing "best when"

Name the working style the version suits, in task terms. Examples:

- "Prefers one task at a time, a short shift, and a fixed route."
- "Likes moving around and being outdoors; comfortable with short customer greetings."
- "Ready for a longer shift and two or three tasks in a set routine."
