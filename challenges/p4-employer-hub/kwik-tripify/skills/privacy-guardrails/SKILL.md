name: privacy-guardrails
description: Privacy, disclosure, and fair-language rules for ERI client and employer work. Use before handling any client information, before writing anything an employer will see, and when describing who a role suits.
---

These rules bind every ERI skill. Apply all of them; a draft passes only when every rule holds. ERI staff confirm items marked **counsel** with a lawyer before go-live.

## Client data in

- Treat every client detail as protected health information (PHI), whether or not ERI is formally a HIPAA covered entity ([HHS](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/covered-entities/index.html)). **Counsel:** confirm covered-entity status.
- Work from the minimum necessary: only the facts this task needs ([HHS](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/minimum-necessary-requirement)).
- Before client data enters a role-design or matching step, replace the 18 Safe Harbor identifiers with a client ID: names, places smaller than a state, dates other than year, phone, fax, email, SSN, record and account numbers, license and vehicle numbers, device IDs, URLs, IP addresses, biometrics, face photos, and any other unique code ([HHS](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/de-identification)). Also drop rare combinations that point to one person, such as a small town plus a named employer.
- Substance use treatment details are 42 CFR Part 2 records: leave them out entirely and flag the file for staff ([HHS](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/regulatory-initiatives/fact-sheet-42-cfr-part-2-final-rule/)). **Counsel:** confirm Part 2 applies.
- DVR records leave ERI only with the client's informed written consent ([34 CFR 361.38](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/34/361.38)). **Counsel:** Wisconsin mental health record law (Wis. Stat. 51.30, 146.82) may be stricter.
- Everything runs on ERI's own machine. If client data may have left it, stop, tell staff, and note the time: breach notice has a 60-day clock ([HHS](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/breach-notification/index.html)).

## Anything an employer sees

- Employer-facing text holds tasks, schedules, and supports only. A diagnosis or disability reaches an employer only with the client's written, specific consent, and only the consented words ([HHS](https://www.hhs.gov/ocr/privacy/hipaa/understanding/consumers/employers.html)).
- Questions to employers stay on tasks, environment, schedule, and supports. Employers do not evaluate or screen applicants by disability, and before an offer they ask only whether a person can do the job functions, with or without accommodation ([EEOC](https://www.eeoc.gov/laws/guidance/enforcement-guidance-preemployment-disability-related-questions-and-medical)).
- Present a carved helper role as a voluntary, negotiated design. The ADA lets employers move marginal functions but never requires them to drop essential ones ([EEOC](https://www.eeoc.gov/index%2Ephp/policy/docs/accommodation.html)).
- Accommodation is an interactive process: offer options, and give the person's own preference first consideration ([EEOC](https://www.eeoc.gov/index%2Ephp/policy/docs/accommodation.html)).

## Describing who a role suits

- Describe **task demands and supports**, never people. Write "this role has low sequencing demand; a picture checklist supports it," not "good for autistic workers" ([JAN](https://AskJAN.org/disabilities/Autism-Spectrum.cfm)).
- Express fit as **support intensity** (light, moderate, intensive) for the role. Diagnoses, autism "levels," and functioning labels stay out of every output; DSM levels are clinical terms for clinicians ([ASAN](https://autisticadvocacy.org/?p=8064), [IACC](https://iacc.hhs.gov/about-iacc/subcommittees/resources/dsm5-diagnostic-criteria.shtml)).
- Every match or fit score is advice to a human job developer. The client's own interests and choices come first ([customized employment](https://ocali.org/customized_employment_guide)).

## Pay and integration

Every role pays at least minimum wage and at least the rate coworkers get for similar work, sits among non-disabled coworkers, and has the same benefits eligibility and advancement chances. A role that fails any of these is rejected, with the reason stated ([34 CFR 361.5(c)(9)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2023-04-05/pdf/2023-07204.pdf), [DWD CIE](https://dwd.wisconsin.gov/dvr/partners/cie)).

## Hours and benefits

Hours and wage choices can change a client's SSI, SSDI, or Medicaid. Send every proposed change in hours or pay to an ERI benefits counselor; the counselor decides.
