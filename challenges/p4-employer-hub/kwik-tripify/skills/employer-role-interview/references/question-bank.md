# Employer question bank

Ask in plain words, one question at a time. Skip any question the employer already answered. Tags in brackets name the role-card field each answer fills. "KT" marks a question traced to the Kwik Trip pattern.

## Business need [business_pain, frees_up]

1. Where do customers or your workflow suffer at busy times? (KT: Guest Services overload)
2. Which tasks pull your key staff away from their main work?
3. Which tasks are often late, backlogged, or done unevenly?
4. If this went well, what would be better in three months?

## Tasks [tasks, excluded_tasks, task_variants]

5. Walk me through a normal day. Name each task in concrete words, like "restock the coffee station."
6. For each task: how often, and what does "done right" look like?
7. Which tasks repeat the same way each time?
8. Which tasks must stay with current staff (cash, keys, heavy equipment, judgment calls)?
9. Which customer-facing tasks could come later, after the person settles in? (KT: register work came later)

## Environment [environment, safety]

10. Can we see the site at a busy time and a quiet time?
11. What is the noise, lighting, crowding, and temperature like where these tasks happen?
12. Where are the checklists, cleaning standards, supplies, and equipment kept?

## Schedule [start_hours, schedule_anchors]

13. Which days and times need help most? (KT: daytime shifts)
14. Can shifts line up with bus or paratransit times? (KT)
15. Can the role start at a small number of hours, for example 10 to 15 a week, and grow? (KT: up to 15 to protect benefits)

## Supervision [supervisor, natural_supports]

16. Who would supervise and give feedback? Who covers when they are out?
17. Is there a coworker who could be a go-to buddy?

## Training [onboarding_plan, coach_access]

18. How do you train new staff today?
19. Can a job coach be on site for the first weeks, and fade out as the person learns? (KT: three-way plan; coach fades with the Store Leader's input)
20. Can we break tasks into steps, with photos or a checklist at the work area?

## Accommodations [selection_steps, accommodations_process]

21. How do you handle accommodation requests at interview and on the job?
22. Are you open to help with paperwork, a working interview, or assistive technology? (KT)

## Safety [safety]

23. What hazards are near these tasks (chemicals, coolers, traffic, lifting, sharp tools)?
24. What safety training and emergency steps apply to every employee?

## Pay and hours [pay]

25. What is the starting wage for this kind of work here? It must be at least the same rate other employees get for similar work.
26. Who on your side approves hours and schedule changes?

## Process, culture, growth [single_contact, check_ins, growth_path, scale_rule]

27. Who is your one point of contact for this role? (KT: one contact)
28. How often should we check in? We suggest every two weeks at first, plus 30, 60, and 90-day reviews. (KT: every 2 weeks)
29. Will you brief your staff before the first person starts? (KT)
30. What job could this grow into? (KT: about a dozen helpers went full-time)
31. Can we start with one person at one site, then decide on more? (KT)
