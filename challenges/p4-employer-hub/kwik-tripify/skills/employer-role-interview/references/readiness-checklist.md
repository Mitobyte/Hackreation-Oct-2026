# Employer readiness checklist

Mark each item **Met**, **Missing**, or **Unknown**, and name an owner for every Missing or Unknown item. The role is ready to post only when every item is Met.

| # | Requirement | Why (source) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | A named single point of contact at the employer | Kwik Trip routes everything through one contact ([DWD protocol](https://dwd.wisconsin.gov/dvr/policy-guidance/services/kwik-trip-protocols.htm)) |
| 2 | A named supervisor and a backup | Feedback and coverage |
| 3 | A written task list with a "done looks like" standard for each task | Clear standards make tasks teachable and checkable |
| 4 | Pay at least the rate coworkers get for similar work, and never below minimum wage | Competitive integrated employment; see the privacy-guardrails skill |
| 5 | The worker works alongside non-disabled coworkers, not in a separate group | Competitive integrated employment |
| 6 | Starting hours and schedule agreed, with room to grow | Kwik Trip starts at up to 15 hours, daytime, fitted to bus times |
| 7 | A posting or requisition route that the person applies through like anyone else | Normal hiring steps ([DWD protocol](https://dwd.wisconsin.gov/dvr/policy-guidance/services/kwik-trip-protocols.htm)) |
| 8 | An accommodation process the employer already uses, open at interview and on the job | ADA interactive process |
| 9 | Job coach access agreed: when on site, and who agrees to fading | Three-way plan; fading with the supervisor's input |
| 10 | An onboarding plan with steps, photos, or checklists at the work area | Systematic instruction |
| 11 | Safety hazards, training, and emergency steps listed | No public Kwik Trip protocol exists; ERI adds this |
| 12 | Staff briefed before the first start | NACS advice on buy-in |
| 13 | Check-in cadence agreed: every 2 weeks at first, plus 30/60/90-day reviews | Kwik Trip BSC cadence; ERI adds 30/60/90 |
| 14 | A growth path named | Kwik Trip helpers moved to full-time roles |
| 15 | Start with one person at one site, then decide on more | NACS advice |
