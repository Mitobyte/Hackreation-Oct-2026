# The Kwik Trip Retail Helper pattern (reference)

Reverse-engineered from public sources, Oct 31, 2026. Use it as a model to copy, not as Kwik Trip policy: the DWD protocol page itself says it may not match current practice.

## Why the role exists

Guest Services coworkers carried many duties, and service dipped at busy times. The helper role takes repeatable, visible tasks off them ([Careerforce MN](https://careerforce.mn.gov/careerforce-blog/vrs-connections-kwik-trip-partners-vrs-provide-meaningful-employment)).

## Duties seen in sources

Store image and sanitation; stocking, unloading, and filling coolers; inventory checks; cleaning gas pumps and the parking lot; coffee and soda stations; basic food prep; maintenance and equipment; register work later. "Duties vary by store." ([DWD protocol](https://dwd.wisconsin.gov/dvr/policy-guidance/services/kwik-trip-protocols.htm), [CSP](https://www.cspdailynews.com/company-news/kwik-trip-recognized-employing-people-disabilities), [NACS](https://www.nacsmagazine.com/Issues/June-2019/eager-work), [RespectAbility](https://www.respectability.org/respecttheability-campaign-spotlight-on-kwik-trip), [Colton](https://www.dwd.wisconsin.gov/dvr/partners/cie/stories/colton.htm))

## Hours, schedule, growth

- Start at up to 15 hours a week so helpers keep benefits; daytime shifts; shifts fit bus schedules ([RespectAbility](https://www.respectability.org/respecttheability-campaign-spotlight-on-kwik-trip), [NACS](https://www.nacsmagazine.com/Issues/June-2019/eager-work)). Hours are individualized: one helper works about 10 hours, another full-time.
- About a dozen helpers had moved to full-time Guest Service jobs by 2019 ([NACS](https://www.nacsmagazine.com/Issues/June-2019/eager-work)).
- Same role name everywhere; task mix, hours, and supports set per person and per store.

## Process (DWD protocol, March 2021)

1. Store Leader asks HR for a helper position. Agencies do not approach stores unasked.
2. HR opens a standard requisition and alerts one DVR Regional Point of Contact.
3. The Regional Contact sends the local Business Services Consultant (BSC) a packet: template, position description, store details, application link.
4. The BSC introduces themselves to the Store Leader and shares the posting with DVR and job-coach providers.
5. Candidates apply online with a resume and a cover letter about skill match, using their own contact details. A signed release of information goes to the BSC.
6. The BSC pre-screens and forwards names.
7. The Store Leader hires through the normal steps: phone screen, references, interview, offer, background check, onboarding. Accommodations are offered at interview and for paperwork.
8. Store Leader, worker, and job coach meet to agree supports, accommodations, and instruction.
9. Supports change only by agreement of all three; the coach involves the Store Leader before fading.
10. Cadence: BSC checks in with the store every 2 weeks; reports monthly; hard-to-fill openings reviewed quarterly.

([DWD protocol](https://dwd.wisconsin.gov/dvr/policy-guidance/services/kwik-trip-protocols.htm), [DWD FAQ](https://dwd.wisconsin.gov/dvr/policy-guidance/services/pdf/kwik-trip-retail-helper-faq.pdf))

## What made it work

- One point of contact; start small (one person, one store); brief managers and staff before the first start ([NACS](https://www.nacsmagazine.com/Issues/June-2019/eager-work)).
- Job coach at the start "helped him learn workplace tasks, get used to the environment, and build confidence"; reliable transport ("I ride Metro paratransit to work, which makes me feel independent") ([Colton](https://www.dwd.wisconsin.gov/dvr/partners/cie/stories/colton.htm)).
- Structure and supportive supervisors and coworkers.

## Outcomes

About 40 helpers in 2013; 200+ DVR placements and a national CSAVR award by April 2018; 370+ helpers by 2019; 500+ helpers in 75% of stores by May 2020 ([WXOW](https://www.wxow.com/news/retail-helpers-program-now-in-75-of-kwik-trip-stores/article_630530ef-e420-5813-8682-73f6818a2857.html), [CSP](https://www.cspdailynews.com/company-news/kwik-trip-recognized-employing-people-disabilities)). Counts vary by source and year.

## Gaps in the public record

No pay rate, no safety protocol, and no formal 30/60/90-day review were found. ERI adds the 30/60/90 reviews.
