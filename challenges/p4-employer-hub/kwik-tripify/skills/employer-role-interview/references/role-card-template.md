# Role card template

Fill every field. Write "open question" with the owner's name when a field is not yet known. The role card describes the job, never a specific person.

```markdown
# Role card: <role title> at <employer>, <site>
Draft <date> by <ERI staff name>. Status: draft for ERI review.

## Why this role
- Business pain: <what suffers today>
- Frees up: <who stops doing these tasks, and for what>

## Tasks
| Task | Done looks like | How often | Can be swapped per person? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

- Not part of this role: <tasks kept with current staff>

## Hours and schedule
- Start: <hours per week>, <days>, <time window>
- Anchors: <bus or paratransit times; quiet and busy periods>
- Growth in hours: <how and when hours can increase>

## Pay
- <rate>, same as coworkers doing similar work. Hours and wage changes go to an ERI benefits counselor before they start.

## People
- Supervisor: <name/role>; backup: <name/role>
- Employer single contact: <name/role>
- ERI contact: <name/role>
- Buddy or natural supports: <coworker role, checklists, visual standards>

## Hiring
- Posting route: <requisition, application link>
- Selection steps: <steps>; accommodations offered: <working interview, paperwork help, ...>

## Start and support
- Onboarding: <day-one tasks, who trains>
- Job coach: <when on site, how support fades, who agrees to fading>
- Check-ins: <every 2 weeks at first; 30/60/90-day reviews>

## Safety
- Hazards: <list>; training: <list>; emergency steps: <list>

## Growth and success
- Next role: <role>; skills to show: <list>; review at: <date or milestone>
- Success measures: <tasks meet standard; retention; coach hours falling>
- Scale rule: <what must hold before adding people or sites>
```
