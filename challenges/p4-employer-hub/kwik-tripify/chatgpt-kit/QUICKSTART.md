# ERI Helper Kit: Quick Start (free ChatGPT)

Turns a job posting into 1-3 small "helper" roles, or preps an employer interview. Everything it writes is a **draft for ERI review**. You check it before an employer sees it.

## Privacy first
ChatGPT free is a public service: no HIPAA agreement, and it may learn from your chats. **Never type or paste anything that could identify a client.**

| OK to use | Never type |
| --- | --- |
| Public job postings; employer names and job notes | Client names, initials, birthdates, addresses, towns, phone, email |
| (Only if needed) a client ID like C-0042 plus plain preferences: "likes sorting, mornings, takes the bus" | Diagnoses, medications, benefit amounts, case numbers, resumes, DVR papers |

Safest habit: make the roles with no person's details, then match the person yourself. If you slip, stop, tell your supervisor, and write down the time.

## One-time setup (2 minutes)
1. Sign in at chatgpt.com. Click your name > Settings > Data controls. Turn **off** "Improve the model for everyone."
2. Each work session, open a **Temporary Chat** (dotted-bubble icon, top right).

## Helperize a job posting
1. New Temporary Chat. Click **+** and attach **ERI-Helper-Kit.md** (unzip the download first; ChatGPT cannot read .zip files). Can't attach? Paste the whole file as your first message.
2. Copy the "Helperize a posting" starter prompt from the top of the kit, paste it in the box, then paste the **text** of the posting under it. Links often don't open, so copy the words.
3. Send. You get a task list, 1-3 roles with variations, and questions for the employer. If it stops early, type **continue**.
4. Read it, fix what looks wrong, copy it to Word, and take it to your supervisor.

**Employer interview:** same steps with the "Employer interview" starter prompt. It asks one or two questions at a time, then fills a role card and a 15-point readiness checklist.

## Get it into Word
- **Signed in to ChatGPT:** type "Give me this brief as a Word (.docx) file." If a canvas or download link appears, click **Download** and choose **Word**.
- **Any account, always works:** click the **Copy** icon under the answer, open **ChatGPT-to-Word.html** (double-click it; it runs on your computer, nothing is uploaded), paste, and click **Download Word file**. You get headings, bullets and tables.
- Quick option: paste the copied answer straight into Word. Check the tables.

## Know the limits
- Free accounts get **3 file uploads a day**. The kit is one upload; use a new chat per posting, or paste the text instead.
- Long chats get forgetful on free. **One posting per chat.**
- ChatGPT can be wrong. Ask: "Run the self-check again and list any problems." Hours and pay questions go to an ERI benefits counselor.
- Out of uploads? Paste **custom-instructions.md** into Settings > Personalization > Custom instructions for the privacy rules in every chat.

## If ERI later gets a paid plan

- **Custom GPT:** creating one needs a paid plan; staff can then use it from a shared link.
- **Skills:** Business, Enterprise, Healthcare and Edu plans can upload the original SKILL.md folders. Free, Go, Plus and Pro cannot yet.
