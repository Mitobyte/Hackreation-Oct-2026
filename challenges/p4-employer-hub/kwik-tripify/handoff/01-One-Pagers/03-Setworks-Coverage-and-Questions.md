# Setworks: what it covers, gaps, questions to ask

*One page. Setworks is the case-management software ERI is evaluating. Everything below is a vendor claim from public pages, checked by two research passes on Oct 31, 2026. The live demo is the real test.*

**Bottom line.** Setworks fully covers none of ERI's 12 problems. It partly covers 6 problems and 9 of 24 ideas. Almost all of that is staff back-office work.

## What it appears to cover

- Reports, dashboards, and case notes.
- Employer and job records, and placement history.
- Milestone rates and invoices.
- Word and Excel templates filled from one client form at a time.
- AI help on notes: feedback while typing, search, drafts with human review.
- Security: HIPAA, encryption, audit history. It lists a BAA and SOC 2 hosting.

## Gaps (not found in public pages)

- Anything workers, families, or employers use. Outside people can only sign forms by email.
- A resume writer, a job-ad writer, or a skills-to-tasks matcher.
- Benefits counseling or Social Security check tracking.
- Bulk document runs or CSV input. API and import are unclear.
- Offline notes at worksites. No native app was found.

Some "not covered" ratings mean "not found". Ask before you assume.

## Top questions for the demo

1. Fill a Word template for 50 clients at once, or from a CSV. Show it.
2. Build a Wisconsin DVR milestone report live from one client's notes. Who builds it, how fast, at what cost?
3. Can custom fields go on job and employer records, not only forms?
4. What can the 2025 custom "AI Agents" do? What does the paid "AI Enhanced" package cost?
5. Is there an API or scheduled export? How do we get all our data out?
6. Is there any benefits or Social Security tracking?
7. What is the full cost for year 1 and year 3? Can we call a Wisconsin reference?

## Where we suggest building

Build what workers and employers touch, and anything Wisconsin-specific. Do not rebuild reports or billing. Keep Setworks as the record of truth. Any helper tool should only read and draft. A job coach approves every output.
