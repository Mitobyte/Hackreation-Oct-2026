# The Kwik Trip-ify skills

*One page. Three small skills that help ERI staff turn a job into small "helper" roles, as Kwik Trip did with its Retail Helper role.*

## What they do

| Skill | What it does | Who uses it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Employer role interview | Guides a job developer through 31 questions with an employer. Then drafts a role card and a 15-point readiness check. | Job developer, with an employer |
| Helperize a job posting | Reads a job posting. Splits it into one to three small roles. For each role: tasks, mental-load demands, support level (light, moderate, intensive), supports, growth path. Each role has Core, Standard, and Plus versions, from 2 days x 2 hours up to 15 hours a week. Flags problems such as low pay. | ERI staff, before or after meeting an employer |
| Privacy guardrails | Privacy, ADA, fair-language, and pay rules. The other two skills load it first. | Loads by itself |

Output is a Markdown and Word brief. Eight real examples are in folder 04.

## How staff use them

- **Claude or another agent.** Load the skill folders. Run on an ERI machine. A local model such as Qwen can work. Say: "Helperize this posting." Paste a link or the text.
- **Free ChatGPT.** Open a Temporary Chat. Attach `ERI-Helper-Kit.md`. Paste a starter prompt and the posting text. Free accounts get 3 file uploads a day. Use one chat per posting. See `QUICKSTART.md`.

## Guardrails

- **No client data.** Never type names, towns, diagnoses, or case numbers into ChatGPT. Use a client ID and plain preferences only, and only if needed. If you slip, tell your supervisor and note the time.
- **Roles, not people.** Briefs describe tasks and supports. They never label a person or guess at a diagnosis.
- **Benefits counselor first.** A benefits counselor reviews every proposed change in hours or pay.
- **Fair pay (competitive integrated employment).** Every role pays at least minimum wage and the coworker rate. It sits among coworkers without disabilities.
- **A human decides.** Every output is a draft for ERI review.

## Before real use

Have a lawyer confirm the items marked "counsel" in the guardrails: ERI's HIPAA status, substance-use records rules, and Wisconsin record law.
