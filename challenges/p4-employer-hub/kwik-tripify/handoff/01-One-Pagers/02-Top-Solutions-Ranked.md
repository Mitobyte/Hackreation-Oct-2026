# Top solutions, ranked

*One page. We scored 24 ideas from 1 to 5 on seven tests. The top score is 60. Source: the ERI report, Oct 31, 2026.*

**The tests:** fit with ERI's frustrations, fit with ERI's wishes, easy to maintain, privacy-safe, easy to demo in one day, cuts paperwork, and builds the resume-ad-match pipeline.

## Our pick

Ideas 1, 2, 3, and 5 form one pipeline. One engine turns data and templates into documents. It also writes the resume and the job ad. Then it matches ads to people. It runs on one Mac. Client data stays there.

## Cautions

- A wrong column mapping can put one client's data in another client's file.
- A model can invent skills. The client must review every letter.
- A model can carve duties that overload a person. The employer must not treat a draft as final.
- A match score must never override what the client wants.
