# What we understood about ERI

*One page. Source: ERI session, Oct 31, 2026. Full detail: 02-Full-Reports.*

**Who ERI is.** ERI is a nonprofit in Madison. It started in 1990. It helps people with disabilities find work, keep work, and keep their benefits. It also trains agencies and helps employers hire.

**The big fear ERI fights.** Many people fear that a job will stop their cash benefits or health care. ERI benefits counselors remove that fear. People, not software, decide if more hours or a raise is safe.

## The problems and who feels them

| Problem | Who feels it | What ERI told us |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Resumes hide real ability | Job seekers with little work history; employers who scan resumes fast | Employers and many families would agree to short task videos, with signed releases |
| Workers need instructions they can use alone | Workers; job coaches who write guides by hand | Most clients do not need a 1:1 coach |
| Workers need to ask for help without a coach on site | Workers; coaches who cover many sites | Some clients may use phones at work as an accommodation |
| Employers want to hire but have no playbook | Employers; ERI employment staff | Employers ask: how many hours? What exactly will the person do? Can we teach this without 1:1 support? |

## Problems ERI did not name, but showed us

- **Paperwork.** About half of staff time goes to reports. Staff fill Word templates by hand.
- **Slow benefit checks.** Social Security checks take weeks or months.
- **Outcome-only pay.** DVR pays ERI only at milestones. Slow cases cost money.
- **Trapped knowledge.** Past job matches sit in a database that staff cannot search easily.

## Limits any tool must respect

- Client data is private. It must follow government contract rules.
- ERI has little money to maintain a new tool. Its in-house team uses PHP.
- ERI may buy Setworks for reports and case notes. A new tool must fit with it.
- Success means fading: the coach steps back, and the person keeps the job.

## What we suggest

Build small tools that cut paperwork first. Then help employers carve good jobs. The Kwik Trip-ify skills (page 4) already do part of this.
