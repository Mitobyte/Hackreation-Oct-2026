# Sample ranking: 11 Madison-area employers

> **Illustrative sample. Public information only. Not verified with employers.** Scores for tasks, bus access, and daytime shifts are my estimates from public sources, not findings. Treat the rank as a way to start a talk, not a verdict.

**How to read it.** Each measure scores 0 to 5. The weights add to 100. Weighted total = sum of (score x weight) / 5, so the best possible total is 100.

| Measure | Weight | What a 5 means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Carvable helper tasks | 30 | A brief or program shows 3 or more helper roles, with a light one |
| B. Metro Transit access | 25 | Site is at a Metro terminal or a campus or Rapid A stop. 1 = outside the Metro service area |
| C. Daytime shifts | 15 | Long daytime hours, or weekday daytime work |
| D. Size and Madison-area footprint | 10 | Many sites or a very large employer |
| E. Inclusive-hiring evidence | 20 | A named, public, long-running program with DVR. 0 = none found in this search (not proof of none) |

## Notes and sources, by employer

### 1. Kwik Trip (total 88)
- **Madison-area sites seen:** Madison-area stores, e.g. 6202 Schroeder Rd
- **Basis:** Retail Helper role (cleaning, stocking, food prep, coffee) started with DVR in 2013; starts at up to 15 h/week; shifts set around transit; about 244 helpers. Store open 6am-9pm daily (Schroeder Rd listing). Bus score: store-by-store route not checked.
- **Sources:** [RespectAbility spotlight](https://www.respectability.org/respecttheability-campaign-spotlight-on-kwik-trip); [DWD Kwik Trip protocol](https://dwd.wisconsin.gov/dvr/policy-guidance/services/kwik-trip-protocols.htm); [Store listing (Uber Eats)](https://www.ubereats.com/store/kwik-trip-1522/RxrhyAFjUKKQx17fNH1wNw)

### 2. UW-Madison (total 71)
- **Madison-area sites seen:** Campus (facilities, dining, mail, and more)
- **Basis:** Largest Dane County employer (10,000+). Campus routes 80-84 and Rapid A stations on campus. No disability-hiring program found in this quick search, but that does not mean none exists. Tasks are an estimate.
- **Sources:** [Top employers (Madison Region)](https://madisonregion.org/about-the-region/major-companies/top-employers); [Metro Transit (Wikipedia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metro_Transit_(Madison))

### 3. Festival Foods (total 70)

- **Basis:** DWD gave Exemplary Employer awards to Festival Foods stores in Kenosha (2022) and Portage (2023), citing work with DVR. Neither is in Dane County. No Madison award found.
- **Sources:** [DWD 2022, Kenosha](https://dwd.wisconsin.gov/press/2022/221012-festival-foods.htm); [DWD 2023, Portage](https://acc.dwd.wisconsin.gov/press/2023/231026-festival-foods.htm); [Public store list (WIC stores)](https://publichealthmdc.com/documents/wic_stores.pdf)

## Cautions

- **Bias.** Only three employers show inclusive-hiring evidence, because I searched only a few public award pages. Employers with a 0 may have strong programs I did not find.
- **Dated lists.** The public store list may be out of date. Hours came from single store pages. Check before use.
- **Bus scores are estimates.** I compared addresses with published route ends. I did not check a stop at each site. Use the Metro trip planner.
- **Example briefs come from other towns.** The Walmart, Best Buy, and Johnson Health Tech briefs were built from postings in Delafield, Brookfield, and Cottage Grove. A Madison posting may differ.
- **No pay score.** I did not find posted Madison pay for every employer. Pay is on the menu so ERI can add it.
- **Not a verdict.** Staff and clients decide where to apply. A rank never replaces the client's own choices.
