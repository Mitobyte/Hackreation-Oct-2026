# Menu of measures

*Pick the ones you care about. Tell me the weight of each (how much it matters). I add or drop measures at any time. Every score is 0 to 5, so employers compare fairly.*

All measures use public information. Where a measure needs ERI's view, such as client areas, you give me general areas, never client data.

| # | Measure | What it tells you | How I find it | Scored 5 when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Carvable helper tasks | How many small, repeatable tasks the employer has | Run the helperize skill on public postings | 3+ roles from one posting |
| 2 | Best support level available | Whether a light-support role exists (light, moderate, intensive) | Same run; read the role ratings | A light role exists |
| 3 | Pay vs minimum and coworker rate | Whether pay meets the fair-pay rule | Posting pay; Wisconsin's $7.25 minimum; ask the employer for the coworker rate | Pay clearly meets both |
| 4 | Metro Transit access | Whether a person can ride to work | Site address against Metro routes and the trip planner | Site is at a terminal or Rapid A stop |
| 5 | Daytime shifts | Whether weekday daytime work exists | Store hours and posting shifts | Long daytime hours |
| 6 | Short-hour schedules | Whether 2 to 15 hours a week fits | Posting; ask employer | Employer already uses part-time helpers |
| 7 | Employer size and sites | More sites means more openings | Public lists, company pages | Many Madison-area sites |
| 8 | Inclusive-hiring programs | Whether the employer already works with DVR or similar | DWD award pages, news, company pages | Named, long-running program |
| 9 | Distance from client areas | Fit with where people live, in general | ERI gives areas (for example "east side"); I match sites | Site is inside the area |
| 10 | Entry barriers | No license, degree, or experience asked | Posting wording | None asked |
| 11 | Customer contact | Level of talk with the public | Role ratings (social demand) | Low |
| 12 | Training and supports | Written steps, a named lead, a trainer | Posting; interview with employer | Employer offers all three |
| 13 | Growth path | Chance to add tasks or hours | Role growth paths | Clear next step |
| 14 | Screening flags | Problems found in the posting (unpaid, commission, adult content, remote, temporary) | Skill screening step | No flags |
| 15 | ERI relationship | Past placements or contacts | ERI staff tell me, with no client details | Strong relationship |

## Suggested starter set

Measures 1, 4, 5, 7, 8 (these are the five in the sample), plus 3 (pay) when ERI can supply coworker rates.

## Suggested weights

Total 100. Example: tasks 30, bus 25, inclusive-hiring 20, daytime 15, size 10. Change them to match ERI's priorities.
