# The offer: rank Wisconsin employers for ERI

*One page.*

**What I offer.** I will run the Kwik Trip-ify skills on a list of Wisconsin employers. You choose the list, or I build one. Then I rank the employers on the measures you care about.

**Why.** ERI staff can see at a glance which employers have the most small, carvable tasks, good bus access, daytime shifts, and a record of inclusive hiring. You spend your time on the best first calls.

## How it works

1. **You pick.** Send a list of employers, or pick measures from the menu. Or both.
2. **I run.** I read each public job posting. The skills split it into helper roles. I record what they find.
3. **I score.** Each measure gets a score from 0 to 5. You set the weights. The total shows the rank.
4. **You review.** You get a table, a CSV file, and the sources. Staff check it before anyone calls an employer.

## Example measures

- Number of carvable helper tasks
- Best support level available (light, moderate, intensive)
- Posted pay against minimum wage and the coworker rate
- Bus access in Madison (Metro Transit)
- Daytime shifts
- Employer size
- Existing inclusive-hiring programs
- Distance from the general areas your clients live in

The full menu is in the next file.

## Rules I follow

- I use public information only. No client data, ever. For distance, you give me general areas, not people.
- I describe jobs, not people.
- I mark everything "not verified with employers" until staff confirm it.

Send me a list, or pick your measures. I start from there.
