ChatGPT to Word

Paste a ChatGPT answer and download it as a Word file with headings, lists and tables. The conversion runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded or saved.

  1. In ChatGPT, click the Copy icon under the answer.
  2. Paste it in the box below.
  3. Name the file and click Download Word file.

Drafts for ERI review only. Do not paste client names or other details that could identify a person.
