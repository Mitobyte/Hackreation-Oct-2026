## Role 1: Clinic Support Helper

Keeps supplies stocked and the non-clinical areas tidy, so hygienists and assistants spend their time with patients.

- **Hours:** 10-15 per week; weekday mornings, for example Wed-Fri 7:30-11:30, set around bus times
- **Pay:** same starting rate as the practice's other entry-level support staff (not stated in posting)
- **Support intensity (of the role):** light
- **Frees up:** hygienists and assistants who restock and tidy between patients
- **Growth path:** add room turnover (b9) after infection-control training

| Task | Standard (what done looks like) | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Restock operatory and front-desk supplies from a par list | every bin at par level on the list | start and middle of day |
| Tidy the waiting room, restroom, and break room | magazines squared, surfaces wiped, trash under half full | every 2 hours |

| time management | low |
| attention | low |
| organization | medium |
| flexibility | low |
| social communication | low |
| sensory load | low |
| paperwork | low |

**Supports the employer agrees to provide**

- par-level list with a photo of each bin
- color-coded shelf labels
- one named assistant to ask
- job coach for the first week, then fading

_Why these tasks belong together:_ both are non-clinical upkeep on one route, with fixed locations

**Variations of this role (tasks and schedules can be mixed)**

- **Core:** Tidy the waiting room, restroom, and break room — 2 h/day x 2 days = 4 h/week total (two weekday mornings) or 2 h/day x 3 days = 6 h/week total. Support intensity: light. Good fit: prefers non-clinical areas, one task at a time, and a short shift.
- **Standard** = Core + Restock operatory and front-desk supplies from a par list — 3 h/day x 3 days = 9 h/week total or 5 h/day x 3 days = 15 h/week total. Support intensity: light. Good fit: comfortable keeping supplies at par with a photo list.
- **Plus** = Standard + Wipe down and reset treatment rooms between patients (non-instrument surfaces, barriers) — 5 h/day x 3 days = 15 h/week total. Support intensity: intensive. Good fit: has completed the practice's infection-control training and wants room turnover. Extra supports: laminated step card in each room; assistant signals when a room is free.

_Schedules above 15 hours a week, and any schedule a specific client takes, go to an ERI benefits counselor first._

## Role 2: Room Turnover Helper (after training)

Resets treatment rooms between patients: wipes non-instrument surfaces and replaces barriers in a posted order, so the room is ready when the next patient arrives.

- **Hours:** 10-15 per week; weekday mornings when the schedule is fullest
- **Pay:** same starting rate as the practice's other entry-level support staff (not stated in posting)
- **Support intensity (of the role):** moderate
- **Frees up:** hygienists and assistants who turn rooms between patients
- **Growth path:** instrument sterilization (b10) only if the practice trains and certifies for it

| Task | Standard (what done looks like) | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wipe down and reset treatment rooms between patients (non-instrument surfaces, barriers) | surfaces wiped in the posted order; new barriers on; room photo matches the 'ready' photo | between patients |
| Tidy the waiting room, restroom, and break room | magazines squared, surfaces wiped, trash under half full | every 2 hours |

**Executive-function demands (role level)**

| Dimension | Demand |
| --- | --- |
| working memory | medium |
| sequencing | medium |
| time management | medium |
| attention | medium |
| organization | low |
| flexibility | low |
| social communication | low |
| sensory load | low |
| paperwork | low |

**Supports the employer agrees to provide**

- laminated step card in each room, with photos in order
- practice's bloodborne-pathogen and HIPAA training before starting
- dental assistant signals when a room is free
- job coach on site for the first weeks; check-ins every 2 weeks

_Why these tasks belong together:_ turnover and tidying share supplies and the same 'ready' standard

**Variations of this role (tasks and schedules can be mixed)**

- **Core:** Tidy the waiting room, restroom, and break room — 2 h/day x 2 days = 4 h/week total (two weekday mornings). Support intensity: light. Good fit: wants to start in non-clinical areas while training for room turnover.
- **Standard** = Core + Wipe down and reset treatment rooms between patients (non-instrument surfaces, barriers) — 3 h/day x 3 days = 9 h/week total or 5 h/day x 3 days = 15 h/week total. Support intensity: moderate. Good fit: trained in the practice's room turnover steps and comfortable with a timed routine.

_Schedules above 15 hours a week, and any schedule a specific client takes, go to an ERI benefits counselor first._
