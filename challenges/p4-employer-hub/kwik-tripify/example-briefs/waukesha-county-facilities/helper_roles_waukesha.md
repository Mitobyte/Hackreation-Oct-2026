## Role 1: Equipment Inventory Helper

Walks assigned buildings with a route sheet, photographs equipment nameplates, and records where each item is, so the facility database fills in faster.

- **Hours:** 10-15 per week; weekday daytime, one building per shift, escorted in mechanical rooms
- **Pay:** within the posted $12.71-$22.52 range for the role
- **Support intensity (of the role):** light
- **Frees up:** the intern and engineers who would walk buildings to collect basic data
- **Growth path:** add data entry (w2), then scanning records (w5); verifying systems and AutoCAD stay with the intern or engineers

| Task | Standard (what done looks like) | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photograph equipment nameplates and record each item's building and room | every item on the route has a clear nameplate photo and a room number on the form | on scheduled building walk-throughs |

| sequencing | low |
| time management | low |
| attention | low |
| organization | medium |
| flexibility | low |
| social communication | low |
| sensory load | low |
| paperwork | low |

**Supports the employer agrees to provide**

- route sheet with floor plans and room numbers
- photo example of a readable nameplate
- staff escort for mechanical and electrical rooms
- one named facilities contact to ask

_Why these tasks belong together:_ one task, one building at a time, with a clear photo standard

**Variations of this role (tasks and schedules can be mixed)**

- **Core:** Photograph equipment nameplates and record each item's building and room — 2 h/day x 2 days = 4 h/week total (two weekdays) or 2 h/day x 3 days = 6 h/week total. Support intensity: light. Good fit: likes walking and moving around, one building at a time, on a short shift.
- **Plus** = Core + Enter walk-through forms into the facility management system — 3 h/day x 3 days = 9 h/week total or 5 h/day x 3 days = 15 h/week total. Support intensity: moderate. Good fit: comfortable entering the day's forms into the system after the walk. Extra supports: form fields in the same order as the database screen.

_Schedules above 15 hours a week, and any schedule a specific client takes, go to an ERI benefits counselor first._

## Role 2: Facilities Records Helper

Scans and files drawings and manuals by building, and enters walk-through forms into the facility system.

- **Hours:** 10-15 per week; weekday daytime at the office
- **Pay:** within the posted $12.71-$22.52 range for the role
- **Support intensity (of the role):** moderate
- **Frees up:** facilities staff who file records and key in data
- **Growth path:** add equipment walk-throughs (w1)

| Task | Standard (what done looks like) | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scan and file paper drawings and equipment manuals by building | each document scanned, named by the building code rule, and filed | weekly |
| Enter walk-through forms into the facility management system | every form line entered; entries match the photos | after each walk-through |

**Executive-function demands (role level)**

| Dimension | Demand |

| sequencing | medium |
| time management | low |
| attention | medium |
| organization | medium |

**Supports the employer agrees to provide**

- quiet desk away from the main office traffic
- supervisor checks the first 20 entries, then spot-checks

_Why these tasks belong together:_ both tasks build the same building records at one desk

**Variations of this role (tasks and schedules can be mixed)**

- **Core:** Scan and file paper drawings and equipment manuals by building — 2 h/day x 2 days = 4 h/week total (two weekdays) or 2 h/day x 3 days = 6 h/week total. Support intensity: light. Good fit: prefers a quiet desk, one task at a time, and a short shift.
- **Standard** = Core + Enter walk-through forms into the facility management system — 3 h/day x 3 days = 9 h/week total or 5 h/day x 3 days = 15 h/week total. Support intensity: moderate. Good fit: comfortable with careful data entry from a form.
