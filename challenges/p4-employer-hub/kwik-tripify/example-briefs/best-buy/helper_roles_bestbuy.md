## Role 1: Display Care Helper (weekday mornings)

Keeps displays straight, dusted, and free of packaging debris during the quieter first hours of the day, so sales associates can stay with customers.

- **Hours:** 10-15 per week; 3 weekday mornings, opening to about noon, set around bus times
- **Pay:** same $15.00-$18.75 starting range as associates
- **Support intensity (of the role):** light
- **Frees up:** sales associates who stop selling to tidy displays
- **Growth path:** add restocking from the backroom (a7), then staging pickup orders (a8)

| Task | Standard (what done looks like) | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Straighten and dust product displays | products squared to the front; no dust or fingerprints on screens | every display twice per shift |
| Clean floor areas and remove packaging debris | no debris or packaging on the floor | twice per shift |

| working memory | low |
| sequencing | low |
| time management | low |
| attention | low |
| organization | low |
| flexibility | none |
| social communication | low |
| sensory load | low |
| paperwork | none |

**Supports the employer agrees to provide**

- picture route card of the departments to cover, in order
- photo of a 'display done right' at each department
- one named associate to ask
- job coach for the first week, then fading

_Why these tasks belong together:_ same route through the store, same 'looks right' standard, quiet morning hours

**Variations of this role (tasks and schedules can be mixed)**

- **Core:** Straighten and dust product displays — 2 h/day x 2 days = 4 h/week total (two weekday mornings) or 2 h/day x 3 days = 6 h/week total. Support intensity: light. Good fit: prefers one task at a time on a fixed route and a short shift. Extra supports: coach on site for every shift in week one.
- **Standard** = Core + Clean floor areas and remove packaging debris — 3 h/day x 3 days = 9 h/week total or 5 h/day x 3 days = 15 h/week total. Support intensity: light. Good fit: ready for two related tasks on the same route.
- **Plus** = Standard + Restock shelves from the backroom — 5 h/day x 3 days = 15 h/week total or 5 h/day x 4 days = 20 h/week total. Support intensity: moderate. Good fit: has mastered display care and wants to add restocking. Extra supports: shelf and bin labels that match, with photos.

_Schedules above 15 hours a week, and any schedule a specific client takes, go to an ERI benefits counselor first._

## Role 3: Curbside Carry-Out Helper

Greets customers at the door and carries verified curbside orders out to their cars.

- **Hours:** 10-15 per week; weekday daytime, daylight only
- **Pay:** same $15.00-$18.75 starting range as associates
- **Support intensity (of the role):** moderate
- **Frees up:** associates pulled off the floor for every curbside order
- **Growth path:** add staging pickup orders (a8)

| Task | Standard (what done looks like) | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carry curbside orders to customers' cars after staff verify the order | order delivered to the right car; handed back to staff if anything is unclear | as orders arrive |
| Greet customers at the entrance | each customer greeted; questions handed to a sales associate | throughout shift |

**Executive-function demands (role level)**

| Dimension | Demand |
| --- | --- |
| working memory | low |
| sequencing | low |
| time management | medium |
| attention | medium |
| organization | low |
| flexibility | low |
| social communication | medium |
| sensory load | medium |
| paperwork | none |

**Supports the employer agrees to provide**

- job coach on site for the first weeks; check-ins every 2 weeks

_Why these tasks belong together:_ both tasks happen at the front entrance with short customer contact

**Variations of this role (tasks and schedules can be mixed)**

- **Core:** Carry curbside orders to customers' cars after staff verify the order — 2 h/day x 2 days = 4 h/week total (two weekday mornings) or 2 h/day x 3 days = 6 h/week total. Support intensity: moderate. Good fit: likes moving around outdoors and prefers short, scripted customer contact.
- **Standard** = Core + Greet customers at the entrance — 3 h/day x 3 days = 9 h/week total or 5 h/day x 3 days = 15 h/week total. Support intensity: moderate. Good fit: comfortable greeting customers at the door between carry-outs.
- **Swap: indoor:** Greet customers at the entrance; Straighten and dust product displays — 2 h/day x 3 days = 6 h/week total or 3 h/day x 3 days = 9 h/week total. Support intensity: moderate. Good fit: prefers indoor work; greets customers and keeps nearby displays tidy.

_Schedules above 15 hours a week, and any schedule a specific client takes, go to an ERI benefits counselor first._
