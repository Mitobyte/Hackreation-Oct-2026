## Role 1: Aisle Recovery Helper (daytime)

Keeps assigned aisles faced, clear of debris, and free of cardboard, so stockers can keep stocking.

- **Hours:** 10-15 per week; 3 weekday daytime shifts, about 9:00-13:00, set around bus times
- **Pay:** same starting rate as other stocking associates (from $15.00)
- **Support intensity (of the role):** light
- **Frees up:** stockers who stop to face shelves and haul cardboard
- **Growth path:** add backroom sorting (c2), then shelf stocking (c3)

| Task | Standard (what done looks like) | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Face and straighten aisles | front row full; labels face out | each assigned aisle twice per shift |
| Clear aisle floors of debris and cardboard | no debris or cardboard in the aisle | every hour |
| Break down empty boxes and take them to the cardboard area | boxes flat and stacked at the baler area | after stocking |

**Executive-function demands (role level)**

| Dimension | Demand |
| --- | --- |
| working memory | low |
| sequencing | low |
| time management | low |
| attention | low |
| organization | low |
| flexibility | none |
| social communication | low |
| sensory load | low |
| paperwork | none |

**Supports the employer agrees to provide**

- map card of the assigned aisles, in order
- photo of a 'faced' shelf for each aisle
- phone timer for the hourly sweep
- one named stocker to ask

_Why these tasks belong together:_ all three tasks follow the same aisle route with clear visual standards

**Variations of this role (tasks and schedules can be mixed)**

- **Core:** Face and straighten aisles — 2 h/day x 2 days = 4 h/week total (two weekday mornings) or 2 h/day x 3 days = 6 h/week total. Support intensity: light. Good fit: prefers one task at a time on a fixed aisle route and a short shift. Extra supports: coach on site for every shift in week one.
- **Standard** = Core + Clear aisle floors of debris and cardboard; Break down empty boxes and take them to the cardboard area — 3 h/day x 3 days = 9 h/week total or 5 h/day x 3 days = 15 h/week total. Support intensity: light. Good fit: ready for three tasks on the same route.
- **Plus** = Standard + Sort products in the backroom by department — 5 h/day x 3 days = 15 h/week total or 5 h/day x 4 days = 20 h/week total. Support intensity: moderate. Good fit: has mastered aisle recovery and wants backroom sorting. Extra supports: department color codes on carts and box labels.

_Schedules above 15 hours a week, and any schedule a specific client takes, go to an ERI benefits counselor first._

## Role 2: Backroom Sort Helper

Sorts boxes from the truck onto the right department carts, so stocking crews can go straight to the floor.

- **Hours:** 10-15 per week; weekday mornings after the truck is unloaded
- **Pay:** same starting rate as other stocking associates (from $15.00)
- **Support intensity (of the role):** moderate
- **Frees up:** unload crews who sort before they can stock
- **Growth path:** add shelf stocking (c3), then unloading (c1)

| Task | Standard (what done looks like) | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sort products in the backroom by department | every box on its department's cart or bay | after each truck |
| Break down empty boxes and take them to the cardboard area | boxes flat and stacked at the baler area | after stocking |

**Executive-function demands (role level)**

| Dimension | Demand |
| --- | --- |
| working memory | medium |
| sequencing | low |
| time management | low |
| attention | low |
| organization | medium |
| flexibility | low |
| social communication | none |
| sensory load | low |
| paperwork | low |

**Supports the employer agrees to provide**

- department color codes on carts and on box labels
- picture guide of which products go to which department
- supervisor sets the order of departments
- job coach for the first weeks; check-ins every 2 weeks

_Why these tasks belong together:_ both tasks happen in the backroom right after the truck

**Variations of this role (tasks and schedules can be mixed)**

- **Core:** Break down empty boxes and take them to the cardboard area — 2 h/day x 2 days = 4 h/week total (two weekday mornings) or 2 h/day x 3 days = 6 h/week total. Support intensity: light. Good fit: prefers a quiet backroom task with no customer contact and a short shift.
- **Standard** = Core + Sort products in the backroom by department — 3 h/day x 3 days = 9 h/week total or 5 h/day x 3 days = 15 h/week total. Support intensity: moderate. Good fit: likes sorting and organizing by color code or label.
- **Plus** = Standard + Stock products on shelves, including freezers — 5 h/day x 3 days = 15 h/week total or 5 h/day x 4 days = 20 h/week total. Support intensity: moderate. Good fit: ready to take sorted carts to the floor and stock. Extra supports: aisle labels that match cart labels.

_Schedules above 15 hours a week, and any schedule a specific client takes, go to an ERI benefits counselor first._

## Role 3: Shelf Stocking Helper (daytime)

Fills shelves in a few assigned aisles and keeps them faced, with older dates in front.

- **Hours:** 10-15 per week; weekday daytime, after the backroom sort
- **Pay:** same starting rate as other stocking associates (from $15.00)
- **Support intensity (of the role):** moderate
- **Frees up:** stockers covering too many aisles
- **Growth path:** add customer greeting (c10), then driver and vendor greeting (c8)

| Task | Standard (what done looks like) | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stock products on shelves, including freezers | shelf full, labels out, older dates in front | daily |
| Face and straighten aisles | front row full; labels face out | each assigned aisle twice per shift |

**Executive-function demands (role level)**

| Dimension | Demand |
| --- | --- |
| working memory | medium |
| sequencing | low |
| time management | low |
| attention | medium |
| organization | medium |
| flexibility | low |
| social communication | low |
| sensory load | medium |
| paperwork | low |

**Supports the employer agrees to provide**

- aisle-and-shelf labels that match the cart labels
- picture guide to date codes
- breaks out of the freezer aisle on a timer
- short script for handing customer questions to an associate
- job coach on site for the first weeks

_Why these tasks belong together:_ stocking and facing share aisles and the same 'shelf full and neat' standard

**Variations of this role (tasks and schedules can be mixed)**

- **Core:** Face and straighten aisles — 2 h/day x 2 days = 4 h/week total (two weekday mornings) or 2 h/day x 3 days = 6 h/week total. Support intensity: light. Good fit: wants to learn the aisles by facing before stocking.
- **Standard** = Core + Stock products on shelves, including freezers — 3 h/day x 3 days = 9 h/week total or 5 h/day x 3 days = 15 h/week total. Support intensity: moderate. Good fit: comfortable stocking a few aisles with date rotation.
- **Swap: no freezer:** Face and straighten aisles; Clear aisle floors of debris and cardboard; Break down empty boxes and take them to the cardboard area — 3 h/day x 3 days = 9 h/week total or 5 h/day x 3 days = 15 h/week total. Support intensity: light. Good fit: prefers to avoid cold aisles; covers aisle recovery instead.

_Schedules above 15 hours a week, and any schedule a specific client takes, go to an ERI benefits counselor first._
